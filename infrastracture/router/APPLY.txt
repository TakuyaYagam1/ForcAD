Router application

From the extracted router bundle, with console access:

  sudo bash install.sh
  sudo bash test.sh

The installer supports Debian 12/13, adds missing prerequisites, applies network
settings, sysctl and firewall, then starts the selected exporters. Network
metric files and the exporter are in the bundle. Add --network-metrics and/or
--game-metrics to enable exporters. --game-metrics also ensures Zeek 9 or newer
at /opt/zeek and creates the forcad-sensor account when needed.

The initial firewall mode is pre-game. Wait until VPN, ForcAD and vulnboxes are
reachable before running test.sh. Use --files-only to stage files without
applying network settings or managed services; prerequisites and requested
metrics packages may still be installed. See COMMANDS.md for option details and
the game-mode switch.
